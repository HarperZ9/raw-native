- **The GPU AO pass walks a BVH** (ROADMAP M3 criterion 1, second step). The renderer's rtao pass uses a BVH built on the CPU (the reference's own build) instead of testing every triangle for every ray. The pass keeps its own triangle test, and a ray whose stack would overflow falls back to the scan. The scan stays as the oracle (`RAW_NATIVE_AO_ORACLE=scan`). On raw-hero and raw-hall the deepest stack is 16 and 19 entries, inside the pass's 64. Bounds: `evidence/m3-gpu-ao-bvh-bounds.json`.
