- **The core's style passes at 4K**: every 64-wide style pass dispatched one group per 64 pixels in one dimension, 129,600 groups at 3840 x 2160, over the 65,535 a dimension that D3D12, Vulkan and WebGPU allow. WARP ran it anyway, so no check saw it. `style_graph::Passes::add` and the other style dispatch sites now fold the groups into rows of 32,768, and every pass in `style.wgsl` indexes `gid.x + gid.y * 2097152u`. The RHI now refuses a dispatch over 65,535 groups in a dimension on every backend (`rhi::kMaxDispatchGroups`), so the mistake fails on WARP too. `raw_native_cli style-4k` runs two style passes over a 4K frame and checks every pixel; it failed before the fix (dispatch refused) and passes after, on WARP in the d3d12 job and on lavapipe in the Vulkan job.
