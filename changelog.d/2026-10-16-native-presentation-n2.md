- **Native presentation** (RELEASE-PLAN N2). `raw_native_present` opens a Win32 window and presents through a D3D12 flip-model swapchain. It handles resize, minimise, borderless fullscreen, vsync on and off, and device removal with recovery on a new device. The frame graph owns the Present: `FrameGraph::presentTo` keeps the passes that draw the backbuffer, and the read-back runs before Present. The RHI gains `Swapchain`, `RGB10A2Unorm`, device loss and caller-owned devices, all additive. On WARP, two runs of 600 presented frames match each other and the offscreen render hash for hash. A one-texel blit offset fails all 600 frames. A run that resizes, minimises and removes the device still matches. HDR10 through the swapchain stays within one 10-bit code of the C++ PQ encode (maximum 0.50 codes over 110,361 channels), and the wrong-exponent control fails. Frame pacing on the RTX 4090 holds its bound: over 10,000 frames at 60 Hz the median interval is 16.663 ms, the 99th percentile 1.09 of the refresh period, and 4 of 9,999 intervals run above 1.5 periods (`evidence/n2-rtx4090/`). Bounds were committed before the first run: `evidence/n2-presentation-bounds.json`.
