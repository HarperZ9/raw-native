- **Frame timing on Vulkan** (RELEASE-PLAN N3). The Vulkan swapchain reports its output's refresh rate from `VK_GOOGLE_display_timing` where the driver offers it; otherwise the presentation host asks the window, which reads the Win32 display configuration's exact rational or the Wayland output's current mode. The run and pacing records carry `refresh_hz` and where it came from, so the N2 pacing bound can be read on Vulkan. Bounds were committed before the code: `evidence/n3-vulkan-timing-bounds.json`.
