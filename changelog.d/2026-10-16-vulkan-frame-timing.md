- **Frame timing on Vulkan** (RELEASE-PLAN N3). The Vulkan swapchain reports its output's refresh rate from `VK_GOOGLE_display_timing` where the driver offers it; otherwise the presentation host asks the window, which reads the Win32 display configuration's exact rational or the Wayland output's current mode. The run and pacing records carry `refresh_hz` and where it came from, so the N2 pacing bound can be read on Vulkan. On one monitor the Vulkan and D3D12 hosts report the same 60 Hz; on lavapipe in CI, X11 reports none and Wayland reports Weston's 60 Hz. The RTX 4090 run on Vulkan (pacing, HDR10, presented hashes) is prepared but not run. Bounds were committed before the code: `evidence/n3-vulkan-timing-bounds.json`.
