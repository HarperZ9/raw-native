- **The AO reference uses the BVH** (ROADMAP M3 criterion 1, first step). The CPU ray-traced AO reference now asks a BVH built over the same triangles, with the same triangle test, instead of scanning every triangle for every ray. On the test scene, a grid of spheres and raw-hero, its AO is identical to the scan's on every pixel; on raw-hero at 64 x 40 it is about 3,700 times faster. The scan stays as a test-only oracle (`RAW_NATIVE_AO_ORACLE=scan`), and the triangle list is still allocated in the arena, so the arena certificate is unchanged. Bounds: `evidence/m3-ao-bvh-bounds.json`.
