- **ReSTIR DI, stage 1** (`raw/renderer/rt_restir.hpp`, `raw_native_cli restir-di`). Direct
  light from many punctual lights by resampled importance sampling and a spatial reuse
  pass with the unbiased 1/Z normalisation, on the CPU. At one shadow ray a pixel, its
  single-frame error in the raw-hall nave with 128 lights is 0.145 of uniform light
  sampling, and its mean stays within the exact reference's interval. Most of the gain
  is the resampling; spatial reuse adds 19% in the hall.
