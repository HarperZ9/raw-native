- **Player lens settings** (`web/shaders/lens/settings.mjs`). Five settings (flare, streaks, halation, fringing, flashSafe) with presets default, reduced-flash (accessibility) and off. They are applied after the time-of-day slot curves; the defaults reproduce the engine look exactly (parameters deep-equal, frame bit-identical, tested). The physical lens controls stay engine-side. Data and API only; no UI.
