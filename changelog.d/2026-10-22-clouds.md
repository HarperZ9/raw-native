- **Volumetric clouds and smoke, stage 1** (ROADMAP P14). `raw/renderer/volume.hpp`: density from soft-sphere lobes
  with surface sub-lobes, a height gradient and Worley and billow erosion; a raymarcher with Beer transmittance, a
  dual-lobe phase, powder, height-weighted ambient and octave multiple scattering; and a delta-tracking path tracer as
  the reference. Single scattering matches the reference within 0.72% (bound 3%); the octave approximation falls 51.7%
  short of path-traced multiple scattering (recorded, and a missed prediction of 25%). `raw_native_cli clouds` renders
  a cumulus, a towering cumulus, a deck from above and a smoke plume at sunset.
