- **Palette-locked lighting** (graphics program, `web/shaders/palettelight`). Light is resolved into the palette's own ramps: a 44-colour palette of hue-shifted ramps per material class (cool and warm variants), a cycling ramp for void pools, short ramps for windows and the lamp. Ramp variant and step come from Bayer 8 x 8 thresholds at two phases, so every pixel is a palette entry. An original night scene (Chapel Yard), a WGSL pass with SwiftShader parity (0 decisions differ in 5 cases; the first run failed at 2.3% to 4.7% from a parameter-packing bug, fixed with bounds unchanged), and a demo video. Against quantising a continuous render with the existing palette dither: ramp purity 1.00 against 0.41, class flips under a moving light 0 against 0.048, pixel changes 5 times fewer. Missed and recorded: the change-rate prediction (the locked pass is calmer than predicted). Not new: hue-shifted ramps, ramp-index lighting with Bayer dither and palette cycling all have prior art (`project-docs/NOVELTY.md`).
