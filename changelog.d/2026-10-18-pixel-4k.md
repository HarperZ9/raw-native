- **Pixel art at 4K no longer comes out wrong**: the splat-buffer clear in `web/shaders/pixel/gpu.mjs` dispatched 129,600 groups in one dimension at 3840 x 2160, which WebGPU refuses (limit 65,535), so the depth and id buffers kept their zeros. It now folds into two dimensions as the post stack does (#183). `tests/web/post_4k.py --page pixel-4k.html` checks it in the post-4k workflow; it failed before the fix (luma sd 0, four validation errors) and passes after (sd 39.7, none).
