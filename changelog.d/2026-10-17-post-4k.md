- **4K renders no longer come out black** (`web/shaders/post.mjs`, `scripts/motion_render.py`). The post stack's encode, pack and unpack passes dispatched one 64-wide workgroup per 64 pixels in one dimension: 129,600 groups at 3840 x 2160, over WebGPU's limit of 65,535 a dimension. WebGPU refused the dispatch, the frames came out black, and the render still reported success. The passes now fold their groups into two dimensions. The render tool now fails a render when the page reports a GPU validation error, or when frames read back at a quarter, half and three quarters of the file have a luma standard deviation under 2 (`--allow-blank` skips that check). `tests/web/post_4k.py` runs dither, film and crt at 4K; it failed before the fix. Found by Night School's 4K narration render.
