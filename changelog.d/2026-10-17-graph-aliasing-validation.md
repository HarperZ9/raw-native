- **Transient aliasing and the RHI validation layer** (ROADMAP M3 criterion 3). With aliasing on (`FrameGraph::setAliasing`, or `RAW_NATIVE_GRAPH_ALIAS=1`), frame-graph buffers whose lifetimes do not overlap share one RHI buffer. A buffer that takes over a slot is zero-filled first, so passes see what a new buffer holds. `RAW_NATIVE_GRAPH_STATS` records the bytes with and without aliasing. Aliasing is off by default. `RAW_NATIVE_RHI_VALIDATE=1` wraps the device in a layer that checks 13 rules (handles, ranges, usages, layouts, workgroup counts, barriers and missing barriers, list and render-pass order, targets, texture copies, read-back). A command that breaks a rule is reported and dropped, and the submission then fails. The negative-control suite covers every rule. Bounds: `evidence/m3-aliasing-validation-bounds.json`.
