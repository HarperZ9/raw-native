- **Raster lighting model 2** (ROADMAP M3 criterion 1 follow-up). With the ray-traced pass on, the frame is now lit by a shadowed sun and by the sky's visibility at any distance: albedo x (0.2 x sky + sun x n.l). Both are traced with the AO reference's own rays, BVH and triangle test, on the CPU and in the GPU's rtao and shade passes. The earlier model, (0.2 + sun) x AO within 2 m, stays as a test-only oracle (`RAW_NATIVE_LIGHTING=v1`) that still renders every byte of the old goldens. Those goldens are kept as `*-v1.json`, and `evidence/identity-golden.json` and `evidence/m2-gltf-golden.json` now hold model 2. AO, its reconcile and its verdict are unchanged. Bounds: `evidence/m3-lighting-v2-bounds.json`.
