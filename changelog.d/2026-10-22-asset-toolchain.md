- **Character asset toolchain, first slice** (`tools/asset`). Brush strokes in a text file become a signed
  distance field, then an extracted mesh, a quad game mesh in three LODs, a UV atlas, baked normal,
  curvature, occlusion and thickness maps, a layered painted albedo, review sheets, glTF and OBJ. Node
  standard library only. The design and the bounds it is held to are in `tools/asset/DESIGN.md`.
