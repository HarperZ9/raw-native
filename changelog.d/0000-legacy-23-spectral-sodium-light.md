- **Spectral sodium light** (graphics program, `web/shaders/sodium`). Low-pressure sodium modelled as the D lines (589.0 and 589.6 nm, plus weak impurity lines), with lights and materials as spectra integrated once into a response table and a deferred shading pass on CPU (float64) and GPU (WGSL). Under pure sodium, eight coloured materials share one chromaticity (spread 0.0001, against 0.64 under a warm window). The protocol was committed first; the misses are kept (the mixing ratio by 0.003, and the distance-recovery prediction by 1.27 m). GPU parity on SwiftShader max 1 code; a wrong-spectrum control fails every case. New scene: Sodium Quay, a harbour street in rain with a lit shop, a van with LED headlights and a painted tile strip.
