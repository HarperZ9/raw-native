- **Story VM** (`raw/story`, layer `story`). Parses line-oriented dialogue scripts (the LWD/1 format of
  Low Water) and runs nodes, conditions, effects, variables, clock, includes and choices, with the
  JS engine's RNG bit for bit. Anything the VM does not own goes to a `Host`. The game layer
  (`raw/game/lowwater`) and `lw_trace_replay` replay a recorded JS run and compare traces byte for byte.
  Work in progress: the rules, world and combat are not ported.
