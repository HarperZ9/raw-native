How attention moves information

A 72-second film drawn live from a model trained on a CPU. Pause anywhere and point at the picture: over the cloud you read any one of its 1,048,576 numbers; over the characters you read where each position attends. Space plays and pauses, the comma and period keys step one frame.

Pause, then point at a dot or a character.

Source: python tools/explainer/attention_model.py --seed 7. The numbers come from attention/data.json and attention/residual.npy; their SHA-256 values are checked when the page loads. The narration is a synthesized voice, made from the author's own recordings; the captions carry every line.
